Refund Policy

The short version: 30 days, full refund, no fight.

Every Family Ready product — the guide, the Action Kit, and each playbook — is covered by a 30-day money-back guarantee counted from the date of purchase.

How to ask. Send an email to familyreadyguides@gmail.com from the address you used at checkout, and tell us which product you want refunded. That is all. You do not need to give a reason, fill in a form, or return anything — the products are downloads.

How you are refunded. Refunds go back to your original payment method, processed through Whop. Timing depends on your bank or card issuer after we approve the refund.

What happens to your access. A refund ends your license to the refunded items and removes download access to them. Items you keep stay yours.

Partial refunds. If you bought several items, you can refund one and keep the others. Each item carries its own 30 days from the purchase date.

After 30 days. The guarantee is closed, but write to us anyway if something went technically wrong (a file you could never open, a duplicate charge). We would rather solve a real problem than hide behind a date.

Contact. familyreadyguides@gmail.com
