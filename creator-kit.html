Money — tracked and paid by Whop

30% commission on THE PRACTICAL SURVIVAL GUIDE — that's 30% of every guide sold through your link at the launch price. The commission applies to the guide; the other Family Ready products carry no affiliate commission.

By Whop, automatically, through your personal link. Every sale made through your link appears in your Whop affiliate dashboard — you can watch clicks and sales there in real time. You don't report anything to us.

When and how do I get paid?
Whop tracks and pays your commissions through your Whop account. Set up your payout details inside Whop so there's nothing holding your money up once the 30 days pass. We never handle your commission ourselves, and we can't adjust what Whop's system records.

The rules — FTC disclosure

Do I have to disclose that I earn a commission?
Yes — every time. US FTC rules require it, and platforms require it too:

  • Put #ad at the start of every caption (not buried in hashtags at the end).
  • Turn on the platform's paid partnership / sponsored label where it exists.
  • In video, say it or show it early — "this is a paid partnership" in the first seconds is enough.

What can't I say?

  • No invented personal stories ("this guide saved my family") unless it's literally true.
  • No safety guarantees — preparedness is a plan, not a promise that nothing bad will happen.
  • No fear tactics, no fake urgency. The only deadline is the real one: the $47 price ends February 17, 2027.
  • No medical claims. (Our separate medical title is not part of this campaign at all.)

What if something in the kit doesn't sound like me?
Rewrite it in your voice — keep the facts, the dates, the prices, and the #ad. Authentic beats scripted, every time.
