About Family Ready

Family Ready exists for one reason: when something goes wrong — a blackout, a storm, a week when nothing works the way it should — ordinary families deserve a calm, clear plan instead of panic and guesswork.

The Practical Survival Guide is our main work: a 2026 edition of civilian preparedness, built from CDC, FEMA, Red Cross, and the U.S. Army field manual FM 21-76, with every claim traced to an official source. It is written in plain language, step by step, for real households — apartments and houses, beginners and the already-prepared.

This is a civilian project. No weapons, no combat tactics, no fear. Preparation, done properly, is a quiet thing: supplies counted, plans written down, everyone knowing their part.

The guide is published in English; Portuguese and Spanish editions are in production.

This content is general preparedness information. It is not medical or legal advice.
