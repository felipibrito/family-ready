Email help

Can't find our email?

It happens — some inboxes file us under Spam or Promotions. Here is how to pull us out, in under a minute.

Gmail

  1. Open the Spam folder (and the Promotions tab, if you use tabs).
  2. Find the email from Family Ready Team and open it.
  3. Click "Report not spam" (on mobile: tap the three dots → Report not spam).
  4. Add Family Ready Team to your contacts — Gmail trusts people you know.

Outlook

  1. Open the Junk Email folder.
  2. Right-click our email (on mobile: select it) and choose "Not junk" / "Mark as not junk".
  3. Add our address to Safe senders so the next lessons land in your inbox.

iCloud Mail

  1. Open the Junk folder.
  2. Open our email and tap "Move to Inbox" / mark it Not Junk.
  3. Add Family Ready Team to your contacts.

Done? Your Day 1 lesson is one tap away.
