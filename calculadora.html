How the numbers are calculated

  • Water: 1 gallon per person per day, split roughly half for drinking and half for food preparation and sanitation (Chapter 4, following Ready.gov/FEMA).
  • Food: 2,000 calories per person per day, the book’s family-table planning rule (Chapter 6).
  • Pets: about 1 oz of water per pound of pet body weight per day; pet food is planned for the same number of days as people food (Chapters 4 and 6).
  • Kit: one go-bag and one flashlight for each person, plus spare batteries in the sizes those devices use (Chapter 3).
Based on THE PRACTICAL SURVIVAL GUIDE — figures follow Ready.gov/FEMA, American Red Cross, and Dietary Guidelines for Americans guidance cited in the book.
