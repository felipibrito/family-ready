FAQ

What format is the book?
A PDF e-book — 1,329 pages, with 518 captioned images and diagrams. It opens on any phone, tablet, or computer, and the checklists are designed to be printed.

How do I receive it?
After checkout through Whop, your download link appears on the confirmation page and is also sent to your email.

Where does the guidance come from?
FEMA, CDC, the American Red Cross, and the U.S. Army field manual FM 21-76. Every claim in the guide is traced to an official source.

Is this a "prepper" or military book?
No. It is a civilian family preparedness guide: no weapons, no combat tactics, no fearmongering. It focuses on safety, supplies, and step-by-step procedures.

I'm a beginner. Is the challenge too advanced?
No. Each day is one short video and one simple action you can finish in minutes, with supplies you mostly already own.

What if the guide is not for me?
Then the guarantee decides, not an argument: every product carries a 60-day money-back guarantee.

Is this medical or legal advice?
No. Family Ready publishes general preparedness information. In an emergency, call 911 and follow official instructions.
