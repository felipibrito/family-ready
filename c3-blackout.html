Blackout & Grid-Down Playbook

Living safely when the power is out — and stays out.

The most common emergency is the simplest one: the lights go out and do not come back on schedule. This playbook condenses the guide's blackout and grid-failure chapter into 75 pages of numbered action cards, checklists, and flowcharts for exactly that situation.

Inside

  • The first hour: light, information, and the checks that come before everything else
  • Food safety clocks — what the fridge and freezer are really doing while the power is out
  • Heat, cold, and carbon monoxide: staying warm and cool without creating a second emergency
  • Water and sanitation when pumps and systems stop
  • Communication when there is no signal — the family plan, on one page
  • Hour by hour, then day by day: the rhythm of a long outage

How is this different from the guide?

It is a condensed visual edition made from the guide's own blackout chapter — the same procedures, rebuilt as numbered action cards, checklists, and flowcharts across 75 pages. The guide gives you the full reasoning and every number in context; the playbook is the grab-and-go version for the night it happens. No new content — the same verified material, faster.
