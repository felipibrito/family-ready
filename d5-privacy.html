Privacy Policy

What we collect. When you join the free challenge, we collect your first name and email address. When you buy, our checkout provider (Whop) processes your payment — we never see or store your card number. We also receive your name, email, and what you bought, so we can deliver it. Like most sites, we use analytics and advertising pixels that record page visits and events (such as a signup or a checkout start) with the origin of the visit.

What we use it for. To send the challenge lessons and the emails you signed up for; to deliver your purchases; to know where each reader is in the challenge so we send the right next step (and stop sending sales emails once you buy); and to understand which pages and partners bring readers, so we can improve them.

Who else touches it. Only the services that run this operation: Whop (checkout, payment, delivery), Brevo (email), Make (connecting the two), and analytics/measurement tools (such as Plausible or Google Analytics, Microsoft Clarity, and the Meta and Google ad tags). We do not sell your personal information.

Your choices. Every email carries an unsubscribe link — one click and the emails stop. You can ask us at any time what data we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it, at support@familyreadyguides.com. Deleting your email data also ends challenge tracking; purchases are kept as transaction records, as the law requires.

Retention. Challenge and email data is kept while you are subscribed and removed on request. Purchase records are retained for tax and accounting obligations.
